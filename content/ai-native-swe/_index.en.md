I’m learning to build software with AI by making small projects and testing their limits. How should a model connect to a system? How much authority should an agent have? What changes when several agents work together, and where should a human step in?

**AI Native SWE** is a series of articles in which I explore these questions through code, error scenarios, and engineering decisions. Together, we will learn by examining either a real-world issue that occurred or a question-and-answer scenario I’ve developed myself.
